parser: dates read month first (#1)

The shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants all write the day first, so a booking for 7 April comes out as 4 July. It reaches every tenant, through the app, by email and through the booking-v2 form. This is the bug #4471 was opened for, and the fix that is queued.

hint: booking-v3 asks for the month first (#2)

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. On a day-first tenant, people type the month first as the hint tells them, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The parser fix does not reach it: only the form does, on booking-v3 tenants.

One-day move: the booking lands a day off (#3)

The desk read these reports as a one-day move (desk-notes.json). They are not a flip: the date moves by one day (“Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday”), and the same meeting falls on different days in Lisbon and Toronto. A day/month swap cannot do that.
